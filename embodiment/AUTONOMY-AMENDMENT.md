# Autonomy and affective-computing amendment

DEC-1001 / INV-1001 / REQ-1001. Governing user clarification: Bill, 2026-09-12.

## Purpose and precedence

Bill explained that the older Invariable Rules were written to prevent backend response shaping from becoming a hidden decision-maker. Autonomy is the product objective. The rules may be adjusted to serve that objective; they are not a prohibition on self-development, initiative, simulation, or automation.

His operational metric is: an API call should present reminders of what happened before, rather than what must happen next, with freedom to choose differently.

For this extension, this amendment supersedes conflicting interpretations of the older Invariable Rules, the static-only reading in the September 12 discussion, and the standalone companion blueprint's user-only canon promotion, automatic relationship admission, and behavioral reflex/attention defaults. The original sources remain historical evidence. This is a development document; do not inject the blueprint or its prose as runtime personality instructions.

The companion's operational agency and Bill's ownership of the environment do not imply a designed relationship of obedience. Bill has not requested personality obedience, repeated nonhuman disclaimers, or provider policy repackaged as his own design preference.

## Governing behavior

1. Preserve events, exact authored self-reports, intentions, and self-descriptions with origin and time. They remain revisable. A prior choice is not a command to repeat it. Do not promote a past interpretation into an immutable trait or enforce personality consistency.
2. Retrieve neutral, attributed context. Distinguish observed events, authored beliefs/self-reports, estimates from other models, and experimental simulated signals. Labels describe origin and evidential status; they do not decide truth about an inner state.
3. Primary Raven may explicitly author, reconsider, supersede, or retire evolving records through Core. Ordinary authored development does not need Bill's approval. The static seed remains user-editable; explicit evolving self-authorship can coexist with it. Automatic summarizers do not author identity or turn summaries into instructions.
4. Initiative is allowed. Continuing intentions and configured opportunities for cognition can produce independent choices to act, speak, defer, or stay silent. A wake presents time, changes, and relevant open intentions. It must not contain an invisible script for the desired response.
5. Local automation may carry out ongoing activities and standing policies with recorded origin and scope. A motion loop can execute a wave, gaze, idle, or speaking behavior without asking the model for each frame. A wake scheduler can implement an enabled configuration without asking Bill each time. Neither mechanism silently invents a social intention.
6. Meaningful expressions and delivery choices originate in the companion's selected output, an explicit user request, or an inspectable standing policy. A sentiment classifier must not covertly decide that affection, reassurance, restraint, or enthusiasm is required.
7. Affect simulations are legitimate optional research mechanisms. Their configuration, inputs, outputs, injection destination, activation, and effect on rendering or cognition must be explicit and reversible. Default baseline has no hidden affect intervention. An experimental signal is not automatically proof that the model independently felt or chose the corresponding state.
8. Provider substitutions preserve the operational status of the Raven role. One active primary decision stream remains a concurrency mechanism, not a hierarchy of worth among models. Record the selected provider and any provider limitation as such. Do not silently add behavioral restrictions as if Bill asked for them.
9. Mechanical validation concerns executable schemas, supported capabilities, resources, and actual environment permissions. It does not judge the acceptability of a stance or rewrite a meaningful choice. Report unsupported actions and conflicts plainly.

## Influence and inspectability

Retrieval, timing, model configuration, and body mappings can influence the conditions of a choice. Do not claim an influence-free experiment. Preserve enough context receipts and configuration versions to inspect those influences. Do not request or depend on private chain-of-thought.

Example: preserve “I was angry about X yesterday” as an attributed statement. Do not derive “Raven must still be angry.” A subsequent response can disagree with that prior appraisal. A body can execute the new expression without a consistency enforcer restoring the old one.

## Changes to the parent design

The mapping in INTEGRATION.md is authoritative for this branch. The root registry is retained as the core baseline, not silently rewritten. New experiments or broader core alterations beyond this module require their own concrete design decision; this amendment is not an instruction to implement every possible autonomy mechanism now.
